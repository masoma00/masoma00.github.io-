01 — About

a little bit about how i think.

I am a final-year Software Engineering student at the University of Central Punjab, Lahore. Problem-solving is genuinely the thing that excites me most — a stubborn bug and a stubborn jigsaw puzzle scratch the same itch.

Most of my recent technical work sits between computer vision and automation: OpenCV, MediaPipe, and Gemini on one side, n8n and Google APIs on the other. Right now that's HumKalam, translating Pakistani Sign Language into text and voice.

Off-screen, I run Hobbies Over Scrolling (community workshops bringing people away from screens) and built YarnOver into a flourishing handmade venture with over 2,000 orders. I shoot authentic stories on my iPhone 16 Plus, edit in CapCut and Canva, and spent winter on a scholarship semester exchange in China.
