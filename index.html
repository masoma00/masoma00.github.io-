01 — About

where logic meets human craft.

I am a final-year Software Engineering student at the University of Central Punjab, Lahore, building at the crossroads of technology, organic media, and real-life communities.

On the engineering side, my focus is computer vision (OpenCV, MediaPipe), gesture translation for deaf accessibility (HumKalam), and end-to-end automation pipelines using n8n and generative models.

On the creative side, I founded and run Hobbies Over Scrolling — a community gathering people away from screens into hands-on workshops — and previously built and scaled YarnOver for 3 years into a high-demand handmade brand with 650+ orders and viral organic reels.

I shoot real, tactile stories on my iPhone 16 Plus, edit in CapCut and Canva, and believe the most loyal audiences are built through authentic, non-AI content.
